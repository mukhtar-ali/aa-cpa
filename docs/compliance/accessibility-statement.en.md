# Accessibility statement (DRAFT: publish only after the final audit, task T033)

Abdulrahman Al-Shubaishiri Professional Consulting is committed to making aacpa.sa usable by everyone, including people who use
screen readers, keyboards, voice control or magnification. We aim to meet **WCAG 2.2 level AA**.

## Conformance status
**⚠ To be completed after the audit:** this site is *fully / partially* conformant with WCAG 2.2 AA. Audit date: ________ ;
method: automated testing (axe) on all page templates in Arabic and English, manual keyboard testing, and screen-reader testing
(NVDA with Firefox/Chrome, VoiceOver with Safari on macOS and iOS). Report: `specs/004-seo-compliance-launch/reviews/a11y-audit.md`.

## What we did
- Arabic is the default language and the whole site is right-to-left aware; English is fully supported. Language and direction are declared on every page.
- All functions work with a keyboard; focus is always visible; the cookie dialog traps and returns focus.
- Colour contrast meets AA; text can be zoomed to 200 % without loss of content; layouts work from 320 px wide.
- Images have text alternatives; forms have labels, clear error messages and no time limits.
- Motion respects the "reduce motion" setting.
